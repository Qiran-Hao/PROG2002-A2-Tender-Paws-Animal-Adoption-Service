Household readiness

  • A quiet room or space an animal can settle in.
  • Everyone at home agrees to the arrangement.
  • Landlord permission if the property is rented.
  • Time each day for feeding, walks and settling in.

Volunteer readiness

  • A regular weekly window you can commit to.
  • Comfort handling nervous or unknown animals.
  • Ability to travel to the shelter or clinic.
  • Willingness to follow the team's safety briefings.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and proof of address.
  • Closed-toe shoes and washable clothes.
  • A charged phone for the team to reach you.
  • A secure carrier or crate if you are fostering.

Next step to register

  1. Choose the service you want from the directory.
  2. Read the service page and note the date and location.
  3. Email the care team to register your interest.
  4. Wait for the confirmation sent in the next project stage.
